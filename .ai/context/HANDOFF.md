Draft rows autosave after a short debounce once date and plan are valid, so quick date/plan/time edits save the final intended value. Rows without a known time are saved to Google Calendar as all-day events, display with a blank time, and print on invoices without a time. The selected plan identity is part of the create request and is not repaired later by UI refresh; if Google Calendar creates the event but refresh fails, the local lesson still keeps that selected plan and event id, and if local save fails the app attempts to remove the created Google Calendar event. Incomplete drafts with no date are preserved across automatic refreshes/activation reloads instead of being replaced by the calendar snapshot. Main lesson row time selectors offer a no-time option plus 08:00 through 20:00 in 15-minute increments while still allowing typed time input. Rapid changes to a reconciled uninvoiced lesson's editable Plan dropdown preserve and save the final selected plan.
- Main-window activation refresh is deliberately deferred and cancelled by the first in-window mouse interaction after focus returns. This preserves the first reactivation click on buttons, arrow icons, fields, dropdowns, pickers, menus, and rows instead of letting background refresh disable controls, close popups, rebuild rows, or clear selection during that click. Activation without user input can still refresh normally.
- Main-window student arrows are occurrence-based, not name-based. They navigate Google Calendar lessons for active students by date/time across the loaded previous/current/next month window, allow the same student to appear repeatedly, select the target lesson, and change the displayed month when the target occurrence is in another month. The student-name dropdown lists other active student short names alphabetically and switches directly to that student without selecting a lesson. Active students' saved standard schedules are fallback occurrences only when the loaded window has no Google Calendar lessons.
- The main window has `Populate Calendar` for the selected student/month. It creates Google Calendar lessons from the student's saved schedule, using today-through-month-end for the current month and the full month for any other displayed month, past or future. Exact same-student same-date/time duplicates are skipped; other conflicts are not detected in v1.
- Write Invoice opens candidate selection, invoice preview, optional price adjustment, invoice reservation, staged PDF verification/publish, issue finalization, print request, dashboard refresh, and read-only invoice viewer. When opened from the main window, candidate selection preselects the currently displayed student when available before falling back to today/time-based invoice candidates. The invoice preview keeps Cancel/window close from hiding an in-progress issue/print operation, shows a busy message, and asks whether to keep waiting if the operation takes longer than expected. PDFs use Danish text, a toner-light header with `studio.example.test`, show the invoice period/month, group service lines by issued plan/price, list included lessons clearly in an indented lesson area that stays in one column through 10 lessons and uses two columns above 10 lessons, include configured bank/MobilePay account lines and payment instructions, use `CVR` as the CVR label, keep the invoice on one page, and add a cut-over note when one invoice covers a mid-month plan change. If staging, PDF writing, verification, or final PDF publish fails before the final PDF is published, the issue flow discards any staged PDF and releases the reserved invoice number so retrying does not skip numbers. If the final PDF has been published but database finalization fails, the issue flow preserves the `Issuing` reservation and recorded published path for retry/support recovery, does not mark the invoice issued, and does not print. Issued invoices carry `IssueStatus` plus `PrintStatus`; normal invoice lists/view/payment paths show only `Issued` invoices. The invoice viewer can reprint an issued invoice from its stored PDF without creating a new invoice number or changing invoice content.
- Invoice numbers display with at least three digits, and final PDF filenames use the invoice number, invoice period, and student full name, for example `Invoice 001 Juni 2026 - Mira Vale.pdf`.
- Invoice `Printed` means the configured printer existed and Windows accepted the print request launch without exception. The app does not monitor physical paper output or Windows spooler completion.
- Invoice VAT applicability is calculated from the invoice period start date, not the issue/print date; this keeps future-month invoices issued before the VAT registration date from being incorrectly treated as non-VAT invoices.
- Documentation refresh on 2026-06-27 corrected the business requirements to describe local `Lessons.Id` as the billing/audit identity and issued invoice lines as the authoritative invoice-to-lesson reference. `Lesson.InvoiceNumber` remains only a denormalized display/lock cache maintained by invoice finalization.

## Safety Invariants To Preserve

- Do not mark an invoice issued unless final PDF storage and invoice metadata save succeed.
- Do not imply physical printing was verified; `Printed` is a successful configured-printer print request launch boundary.
- Do not silently overwrite an issued invoice PDF.
- Do not consume or skip an invoice number, or leave a staged PDF behind, when the issue flow fails before final PDF publication.
- After final PDF publication, do not release/delete the `Issuing` reservation because finalization failed; preserve the published path for retry/support recovery and avoid printing.
- Do not allow invoice layout changes to push payment details, totals, or footer text to a second PDF page; longer lesson lists must fit the one-page invoice layout by using the available middle lesson area.
- Do not silently edit issued invoices.
- Invoiced lessons are not editable.
- Do not infer invoiced/completed/paid lesson state from matching student/date/time/plan data. Invoice state belongs only to the durable local lesson identity linked to an issued invoice.
- Do not let Cancel/window close hide an invoice issue/print operation that is still in progress; extended-wait prompts do not mean the operation was safely cancelled.
- Issued invoice lines must reference exact local lessons through `LessonId`; the database enforces missing-reference, duplicate-reference, and referenced-lesson delete constraints.
- Do not invent local lesson identity for legacy invoice-line migration. If an old line cannot be safely linked, warn clearly and continue with invoice headers plus safely linkable lines.
- Completed lessons can only be toggled back to Planned after confirmation; while Completed, no date/time edit or delete is allowed.
- Google Calendar write failures must be visible immediately and must not pretend local state changed.
- Google Calendar write payloads must use IANA time zone IDs, not raw Windows time zone IDs.
- Automatic refresh must preserve incomplete lesson draft rows.
- Activation/focus recovery must not consume the first user click or invalidate controls, popups, row selection, menus, or fields during that click.
- Populate Calendar must not create exact duplicate same-student same-date/time lessons; past-month backfill is allowed and seeds the whole displayed month.
- OAuth tokens must remain Windows-protected, use collision-resistant token-key filenames, and must not be stored in SQLite or plaintext config. Do not launch interactive Google OAuth from startup or automatic refresh; use the explicit Settings Connect command for browser authorization.
- Do not restore the old user-selected Google OAuth JSON setup flow unless Ruben explicitly asks for a migration fallback.
- Do not allow Draft or Retired catalog plans to be assigned to students.
- Do not mutate existing student plan period commercial-term snapshots or issued invoices when a catalog plan changes lifecycle state or display name.
- Do not derive old invoice display text from current plan catalog names or rename history; issued invoice line snapshots are authoritative for invoice text.
- Deactivating/reactivating a student must not mutate plan periods, schedule entries, or issued invoices.
- Schema changes must be implemented as EF migrations and verified against fresh and current-schema SQLite databases.
